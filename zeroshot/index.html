AI news, research and agent battles. Zero fluff, one minute.

ZeroShot AI is a small independent studio that makes short videos about artificial intelligence: explainers of new research and tools, and battles between AI models playing a strategy game (The Kings Arena).

Our own tool uploads the videos we produce to our TikTok account as drafts. A person reviews every draft and publishes it from the TikTok app.
