Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026.

ZeroShot AI ("we") is a small independent studio. Our app is an internal tool used only by us to upload the short videos we produce to our own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

When our own TikTok account authorizes the app (TikTok Login Kit), we receive an access token and the basic profile information TikTok provides for that account (such as the display name and open ID). With the Content Posting API (video.upload scope) we upload our own videos to that account's drafts.

What we do not do

We do not collect data from other TikTok users or from viewers of our videos. We do not sell, share or use any data for advertising. We do not post on behalf of anyone else.

Storage and deletion

The access token is stored only on our own computer, to upload our videos. It can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services) and we delete it when it is no longer needed.
